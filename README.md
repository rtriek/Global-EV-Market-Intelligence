# 🌍 Global EV Market Intelligence

> **Market Research Agent + Amazon QuickSight Dashboard**  
> A data-driven analysis of the global passenger-car electric vehicle (EV) market from **2010–2023**, combining AI-assisted market research with interactive business intelligence.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes the global passenger-car EV market to identify the most important **market growth, adoption, powertrain, and geographic trends** that can support business and investment decisions.

The project combines:

- 🤖 **AI-assisted market research**
- 📊 **Amazon QuickSight data visualization**
- 📈 **Historical EV market analysis**
- 🧭 **Geographic and powertrain comparisons**
- 📝 **A structured Market Intelligence Research Brief**

The analysis focuses on historical evidence from **2010–2023**, with **2023** used as the latest-year comparison.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Assess the global passenger-car EV market using historical **EV sales, sales share, stock, powertrain, and geographic data** to identify major market trends and decision-relevant signals.

The findings are intended to support:

- Market prioritization
- Geographic expansion decisions
- EV investment opportunities
- BEV-oriented market strategy
- Understanding of powertrain dynamics

---

## 📌 Key Findings

### 1. 🌎 The global EV market reached significant scale

The dataset records approximately **383.42M total EV sales across 2010–2023**, with a strong long-term upward trend.

**Business implication:** EVs have moved beyond a niche market and represent a significant automotive market opportunity.

### 2. 📈 EV adoption accelerated strongly

Global EV sales share reached approximately **18% in 2023**.

**Business implication:** EVs had become an important component of the new passenger-car market by 2023.

### 3. 🔋 BEVs dominate the powertrain mix

The powertrain analysis shows **BEVs as the largest component** of EV sales compared with PHEVs and FCEVs.

**Business implication:** BEV-focused strategies are particularly relevant when evaluating EV market opportunities.

### 4. 🗺️ EV sales are geographically concentrated

The 2023 Top 5 country view identifies:

**China · USA · Germany · France · United Kingdom**

as the leading countries in the selected EV-sales view.

**Business implication:** Market-entry and expansion strategies should prioritize leading markets while accounting for local market differences.

## 🔍 Research Scope

### Included

- Global passenger-car EV market
- Historical period: **2010–2023**
- EV sales and sales share
- EV stock
- BEV / PHEV / FCEV powertrains
- Geographic market comparison
- Leading countries in 2023

### Not Included

The dataset does not contain:

- Manufacturer/OEM-level competitive data
- Vehicle pricing
- Charging infrastructure
- Policy/incentive variables
- Profitability
- Consumer demographics
- Forecast variables

Therefore, the project is designed primarily for **descriptive historical market intelligence**, not causal modeling or sales forecasting.

---

## 🧪 Research Approach

The project followed an AI-assisted market research workflow:

1. **Define the business objective**
2. **Identify the key market questions**
3. **Analyze the historical EV dataset**
4. **Prioritize market scale, adoption, powertrain mix, and leading markets**
5. **Build the Amazon QuickSight dashboard**
6. **Synthesize the findings into a Market Intelligence Research Brief**
7. **Assess confidence, limitations, and strategic implications**

### Main Research Questions

- How did global EV sales evolve from 2010 to 2023?
- How did EV sales share change over time?
- Which powertrain types dominate the market?
- Which countries lead EV sales in 2023?
- What business decisions can the evidence support?

---

## 💡 Strategic Implications

The analysis can support decisions around:

- **Geographic prioritization** — identifying the strongest EV markets
- **Market expansion** — comparing mature and emerging opportunities
- **Powertrain strategy** — understanding the relative importance of BEVs, PHEVs, and FCEVs
- **Investment prioritization** — using market scale and adoption as decision signals
- **Market monitoring** — tracking how EV adoption evolves over time

### Important Caveats

The analysis should **not** be interpreted as:

- Manufacturer-level market share or performance
- Revenue or profitability analysis
- Proof that a specific policy or price change caused EV growth
- A forecast of future sales or market share

Confidence is **high for descriptive historical findings** and more limited for forward-looking implications because the dataset ends in 2023 and does not contain causal or forecast variables.

## 📈 Dashboard Design

The dashboard was intentionally kept focused rather than overloaded with visuals.

### KPI Layer
Three high-level indicators provide an immediate market snapshot:

**383.42M** total EV sales · **18%** 2023 sales share · **40.07M** EV stock

### Trend Layer
Two time-series views show:

- How EV sales developed by powertrain
- How EV sales share changed over time

### Geographic Layer
A Top 5 donut chart highlights the leading countries in the 2023 EV-sales view.

This structure moves from **market size → market trend → geographic concentration**, making the dashboard suitable for a quick executive read.

## ⭐ Final Takeaway

The global passenger-car EV market underwent a major transformation between **2010 and 2023**. EV sales reached substantial scale, adoption climbed to **18% of new-car sales in 2023**, BEVs remained the dominant powertrain, and a relatively small group of countries accounted for a large share of the market.

The project turns these findings into an executive-friendly dashboard and research brief designed to help decision-makers understand **where the market is growing, how the technology mix is evolving, and which geographic markets deserve attention**.
